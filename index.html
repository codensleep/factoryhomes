Overview

How to get a house in 90 days.

The process is built around parallel execution. While the home is being manufactured, the site is prepared, utilities are aligned, and approvals are pushed through on one coordinated schedule.

Days 1-7

Choose the model and confirm the site

Select the home, finalize the configuration, review parcel conditions, and lock scope, pricing, and the deployment plan.

Days 8-21

Engineering, permits, and utility planning

Complete technical drawings, submit required approvals, and map utility, access, and foundation requirements.

Days 22-60

Build in the factory while site work starts

Manufacture the home in a controlled environment as crews prepare the foundation, trenching, grading, and connections.

Days 61-75

Transport, set, and install

Deliver the home, place it on site, complete assembly, and connect core systems for final commissioning.

Days 76-90

Inspect, finish, and hand over

Close out final inspections, complete punch-list items, test systems, and deliver a move-in-ready house.
